The first release of Pollymetric: your Mac's health at a glance, in the menu bar.

### What you get

- **A health score in your menu bar** that only asks for attention when something needs you.
- **What's slowing your Mac down, by name:** "next dev in storefront", not "node", grouped by the app that started it.
- **Two weeks of history**, so the thing that spiked for twenty seconds and vanished is still on the record, and anything that keeps coming back is flagged.
- **Explain and Find a Fix:** hand any process to your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex and others) and get the answer beside it. Nothing changes without your okay.
- **Free up space safely** from caches and old projects, seeing exactly what goes first.
- **Startup items** from unknown developers flagged, and a **one-click security check**.
- **Connect your AI agents** so they can check on your Mac, approved with Touch ID and revocable any time.
- **Private by design:** everything stays on your Mac.

### Install

Download **Pollymetric.dmg** below, open it, and drag Pollymetric into Applications. A short setup takes it from there.

> **Early build:** this version isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Notarized builds that open without this step are coming.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
